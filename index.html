Getting there

Earthly Pleasures sits in the Belgrave township, at the mountain end of the Burwood Highway.

Parking

There is plenty of parking nearby, including small car parks off the main street, Alexanders carpark behind Woolworths, and a multi-story carpark at Belgrave train station.

By train

Belgrave is the end of the Belgrave train line. Earthly Pleasures is a short walk from the station.

chapter five

Make a day of it

A 5.30pm ceremony leaves you with a whole springtime Saturday at your disposal. Here are some suggestions for how you might spend it:

During the day

Cameo Cinemas gentle

An independent cinema directly opposite the wedding venue.

Puffing Billy Railway gentle

The famous steam train departs from Belgrave station. A lovely family outing. Bookings are required.

Grants Picnic Ground gentle

A nice spot to have a picnic underneath some looming Mountain Ash trees. There are often Rainbow Lorikeets around. Several easy walking trails lead directly off from the picnic grounds.

Sherbrooke Forest walks moderate

Fern gullies, lyrebirds if you're lucky and quiet, and a walking track to Sherbrooke Falls (a tiny but pleasant waterfall).

The 1000 Steps ambitious

The Kokoda Track Memorial Walk at Ferntree Gully, a short drive from Belgrave. Undertake it only if you intend to earn your dinner emphatically.

Treetops Adventure ambitious

Ziplines, rope bridges and tightropes strung through the forest on Old Monbulk Road. Courses begin at age three. Bookings are required.

Boulder Lab ambitious

Indoor bouldering (a kind of rock climbing) at Ferntree Gully. Julian and Liesl have spent a lot of time here, falling off colourful shapes stuck to walls. Kids aged 12+ can climb. The bathrooms have showers, so you can rinse off before the reception.

And just before the ceremony

Cognoscenti Food & Wine civilised

Directly across the road from the venue. They open at 5pm, which leaves just enough time for one fancy drink before the ceremony.

Micawber Tavern civilised

A century-old pub on Monbulk Road, open from 11am on Saturdays. Some lovely walking trails lead off the carpark, should you wish to earn the drink first.
